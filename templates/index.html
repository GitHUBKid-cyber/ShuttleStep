We use the player's height as a ruler to measure distances in metres.

Analysis takes about as long as the video itself. Keep this page open.

For the best results

  1. Keep the phone still, behind the back line, at head height or higher.
  2. Make sure the player's whole body, feet included, stays in the frame.
  3. Film in landscape with good light, and keep other people out of that half of the court.
  4. Clips of 30 seconds to 2 minutes work best.
